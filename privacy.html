2. What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your company or website, if you give one

  • the date and time you sent it, your browser type, and the page you came from

Like almost every website, the company that hosts this site keeps short-lived technical logs of each visit: your IP address, the time and the page requested. We don't use these to identify you.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't buy or receive data about you from anyone else.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss possible work with you. This is a step you've asked us to take before a possible contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep a record of enquiries and send you an automatic confirmation that your message arrived. This is our legitimate interest in running the business and following up properly (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the site and form secure and filter out spam. Also a legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). Submissions that our spam checks flag are discarded and not stored.

We will never add you to a mailing list, sell your data, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who handles your data for us

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the website and the contact form. They process data only on our instructions.

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the website and the service that receives the contact form (United States).
  • Hostinger provides the server in the United Kingdom that runs our workflow-automation software, which passes your enquiry on to the services below.
  • Google provides the email (Gmail) used to notify us and send your confirmation, and the spreadsheet (Google Sheets) where enquiries are recorded.
  • Google Fonts, Cloudflare (cdnjs) and jsDelivr deliver the fonts and code libraries this page uses. Like any site you load, they receive your IP address when your browser requests these files.

We may also disclose data if the law requires us to.

Some of these providers, notably Vercel and Google, may process data in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK–US data bridge (for certified providers) or by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, which require your data to be protected to UK standards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted 12 months after our last contact with you.
  • If you become a client, we keep what we need for the work and for our legal and tax obligations. This is typically 6 years after the end of the engagement.
  • Hosting logs are kept only for the short period set by our hosting provider.

7. Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't set cookies. When you choose a language (EN or BG), your browser remembers that choice in its local storage, so the site opens in the same language next time. It isn't used for anything else, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

To use any of these rights, email stoycho.lyubenov@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month. It's free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.
